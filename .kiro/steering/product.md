# Product Overview — SamForGeophysics

## Positioning

A **Frankenstein**-category entry: fuse a vision foundation model (Segment Anything Model, SAM) with seismic interpretation, a domain it was never trained for, to make weak deep reflectors interpretable.

Slogan: *"With lower frequencies and a cleaner platform, reach for deep targets once deemed unhearable — quiet yourself first, then hear the Earth."*

## Product goal

Ship a **demonstrable product**, not a proof of concept. A user loads a seismic section, runs low-frequency-first enhancement, clicks point/box prompts, gets SAM segmentation of faults, horizons and deep targets, watches a self-improving interpretation loop refine the prompts, and exports the result.

## Differentiator — state it precisely

- Low-frequency content carries deep information; a cleaner (low-noise) platform reduces the noise floor. Together they make previously unhearable deep targets interpretable.
- A **self-improving interpretation loop**: score label-free QC metrics (deep-target edge leakage and envelope contrast, fault continuity) after each SAM run and automatically refine prompts for up to 3 rounds. Ground-truth overlap is shown for reference only and never drives the loop.

### Do NOT claim

- Never claim "first SAM for seismic" or any novelty of applying SAM to seismic data. Prior work exists (see `#sources`).
- Never present UAV hardware or quantum sensing as implemented. They are **background narrative only**: "cleaner platform" means low-noise data conditioning in this software, not a physical vehicle or sensor.
- Never invent accuracy numbers, benchmarks, speedups or dataset sizes. Every quantitative statement must come from a reproducible measurement in this repo, or be explicitly labelled `conceptual` / `UNVERIFIED`.

## Audience and tone

Geophysicists and hackathon judges. Technical, honest, concise. Prefer showing a working interaction over claiming performance.

## Data policy

Public or fully synthetic data only. No proprietary or licensed field data, no model weights, no SEG-Y volumes in the public repository. All demo data is generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_seismic.py` with a fixed seed.
